Some parents or guardians who are seeking asylum may be eligible for free school meals under separate asylum support arrangements.

These families may not have a National Insurance number and cannot be processed through the standard online free school meals check.

Where the parent or guardian is an asylum seeker, the local authority must use the approved offline process instead of the online National Insurance check.

Accessing the offline process

Local authorities must use the approved offline free school meals application process for asylum seeker cases.

This process is separate from the online check and should be used only when the parent or guardian does not have a National Insurance number or is claiming asylum support.

Access the official free school meals asylum support form (authorised users only)

When to use the offline process

Use the offline process when the parent or guardian:

  • is seeking asylum
  • does not have a National Insurance number
  • is claiming asylum support
  • needs eligibility to be assessed under the asylum support route

How local authorities should support families

You must:

  • provide information about the offline asylum support route
  • offer assistance to families who may need help completing the form
  • request supporting evidence confirming asylum support status
  • ensure that the form is completed and submitted through the approved process
  • communicate the outcome of the asylum support check to the family in a timely manner

Submitting and storing the evidence

Local authorities must ensure the asylum support form is completed and stored securely. This includes:

  • using the approved submission process
  • keeping a copy of the completed form and supporting evidence
  • storing all documents securely to protect the family's privacy
