You can apply for free school meals at All Saints CofE Primary school, Ilkley, LS29 8QH through your local authority website.

To support your claim for free school meals, you can provide:

  • up to three of your latest Universal Credit statements
  • a letter of Entitlement for income-related Employment and Support Allowance (ESA)
  • a letter of Entitlement for the guaranteed element of Pension Credit
  • documents showing you are receiving relevant support under part VI of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999
