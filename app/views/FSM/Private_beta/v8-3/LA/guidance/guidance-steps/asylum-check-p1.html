Some parents who are claiming asylum are eligible for free school meals under separate eligibility criteria. These families may not have a National Insurance number and cannot be processed through our standard online free school meals checking system.

To complete checks, local authorities should:

  • review supporting evidence to check an applicant with an asylum support reference number (formerly NASS number)
  • only use the approved application form for asylum seekers if the applicant has been unable to provide supporting evidence for the local authority to check

Reviewing supporting evidence

An applicant with an asylum support reference number (formerly NASS number) needs to provide a document proving they are receiving relevant support under part VI of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999. This may include:

  • a letter from the Home Office
  • a visa or student visa
  • other kinds of documentation, to be reviewed at your discretion

If the document provided demonstrates that they receive such support, this is sufficient evidence for them to claim free school meals for their children.

There is another subset of failed asylum seekers supported under Section 4 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, whose children may also be eligible for free school meals. For guidance, read providing free school meals to families with no recourse to public funds (NRPF) on GOV.UK.

Accessing and completing the approved application form

Local authorities can use the approved offline free school meals application form for asylum seekers if the applicant has been unable to provide supporting evidence.

This document is classified as OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE. Only authorised local authority staff should access and complete it.

Access the official free school meals application form (authorised users only)

Where to send the completed form

Completed forms should be sent to:

ICESSVECWorkflow@homeoffice.gov.uk

When to use the offline form

Use the offline form when the parent or guardian:

  • is seeking asylum
  • does not have a National insurance number
  • cannot be verified through the online system
  • meets the free school meals criteria under asylum support arrangements

How local authorities should support families

You must:

  • request supporting evidence confirming asylum support status
  • provide information about the offline form if the applicant cannot provide supporting evidence
  • ensure that the completed form is submitted to the Home Office for confirmation
  • communicate the outcome of the application to the family in a timely manner

Submitting and storing the form

Local authorities must ensure that the offline form is submitted securely and stored appropriately. This includes:

  • using secure methods to submit the form to the Home Office
  • keeping a copy of the submitted form and any supporting evidence
  • storing all documents securely to protect the family's privacy

Help and support

For further help and support, contact our support team using the Customer help portal.
